*Halden Pharma is a fictional company. This document is demonstration content for FundingEnvoy.*

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This procedure sets out how Halden Pharma receives, reviews, decides, contracts, pays, reconciles, closes and discloses requests for independent medical education grants and charitable contributions.

1.2 It puts the Grants and Donations Policy v6 ("the Policy") into practice. Where this procedure and the Policy differ, the Policy prevails and the process owner corrects this procedure.

1.3 The procedure exists so that every request is decided on its merits, by people who have no commercial interest in the outcome, against criteria that are written down before the request arrives, and so that the record shows this.

## 2. Scope and definitions

2.1 This procedure covers two request families:

- **Independent medical education**, accredited (the provider is accredited and offers credit) and non-accredited (no credit, or a provider that is not accredited).
- **Charitable contributions** to registered non-profit organizations.

2.2 It does not cover research grants, investigator-initiated studies, sponsorships with a commercial return (exhibits, booths, satellite symposia), memberships, product donations or patient-assistance donations. Those follow their own procedures. A request that turns out to be one of these is closed here and redirected.

2.3 Definitions:

- **Request**: a request for funding made by an organization through the requestor portal.
- **Requestor**: the person at the organization who prepares and submits the request. The requestor acts for the organization, never for themselves.
- **Authorized signer**: a person named in the organization's profile who can sign agreements for it.
- **FundingEnvoy**: the assistant built into the requestor portal and the grants desk. It prepares, checks and drafts. It does not decide.
- **Rulebook**: the published set of checks FundingEnvoy and the grants desk run on every request. Each check names the Policy or SOP clause, code or law it comes from.
- **Committee**: the Grants and Donations Committee described in 6.2.
- **Reviewing group** (triage group): the team that owns a request after triage, for example US medical education.
- **Business day**: Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays of the country of the reviewing group.

## 3. Intake

3.1 **Requests come from organizations, through the portal.** A request is made by an eligible organization (Policy 3.1) through the Halden requestor portal. Requests by email, by letter or handed to a Halden employee are not accepted; the grants office replies with the portal link. FundingEnvoy may help the requestor prepare the request: classify it, read the documents, propose values with their source, and say what is still missing. Every value it proposes is confirmed by the requestor before it is saved as confirmed. The requestor types every statement (attestation) and answers every conflict of interest question personally.

3.2 **Required documents.** Every request includes a request letter on letterhead signed by an authorized signer, an itemized budget, and the organization's most recent annual financial statement. Education requests also include a prospectus or agenda and the provider's conflict of interest policy; accredited education includes the accreditation statement. Country documents are listed in the catalogue: for US organizations a W-9 and, for associations, patient organizations and charities, proof of non-profit status; for UK and French charities and associations, proof of registration. Documents already held in the organization's profile may be reused if they are current.

3.3 **The request type is confirmed before the form is fixed.** FundingEnvoy proposes the request type from the requestor's description and documents and explains what follows from it. The requestor confirms the type. If the coordinator later finds the type is wrong, the coordinator changes it and the checks re-run.

3.4 **Accuracy.** The requestor confirms that the request is accurate and complete and that they reviewed every value FundingEnvoy proposed. A request that is later found to be materially inaccurate may be declined or, if already approved, suspended under 7.5.

3.5 **One request per activity.** An organization makes one request per activity or programme. Requests split to stay under a threshold (for example the single-approver limit in 6.5) are treated as one request.

## 4. Timing

4.1 **Lead time.** Requests are submitted at least 60 calendar days before the activity starts. This is the time Halden needs for review, a committee decision and an executed agreement, all of which must come before the activity (7.1). FundingEnvoy shows the lead time while the request is prepared. A request with less than 60 days may still be submitted; it carries a flag the reviewer sees, and the committee decides whether there is time to do things properly. Shorter lead times are never a reason to skip a step.

4.2 **Changes of scope.** A change to dates, format, location, audience or budget after approval is requested in writing at least 30 days before it takes effect, or as soon as the organization knows of it. The coordinator decides whether the change needs a new committee decision.

4.3 **No retrospective funding.** Halden does not fund an activity that has started or a cost already incurred. A request whose start date is in the past cannot be submitted.

## 5. Screening, completeness and triage

5.1 **Screening.** Every organization is screened at intake and again before each payment. US organizations are screened against the HHS-OIG List of Excluded Individuals and Entities, SAM.gov exclusions, the OFAC SDN list and, for tax-exempt organizations, the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search. Organizations outside the US are screened against the OFAC, UK OFSI and EU consolidated sanctions lists, and their officers against politically exposed persons data. A possible match is adjudicated by Compliance before the request goes further; a confirmed match ends the request. Screening results older than 90 days are re-run.

5.2 **Triage.** Each request is routed to a reviewing group by its type, its country and the geography of its audience. The current routing is:

| Request | Country | Audience | Reviewing group |
|---|---|---|---|
| Education, accredited or non-accredited | US | Mainly US | US medical education |
| Education | US | Not mainly US, or international | Global grants committee |
| Education | UK | Not international | UK medical education |
| Education | France | Not international | France medical affairs |
| Education | Any | International | Global grants committee |
| Charitable contribution | US | Any | US charitable contributions |
| Charitable contribution | UK | Any | UK charitable contributions |
| Charitable contribution | France | Any | France affiliate |

FundingEnvoy proposes the reviewing group; the coordinator confirms it or overrides it with a reason, and the override is recorded.

5.3 **Completeness.** A request is complete when every check in the rulebook marked "must fix" passes. FundingEnvoy runs the rulebook on every change while the request is prepared and again at submission, and attaches a pre-submission review summary to the request. The coordinator claims the request within two business days of submission and starts from the summary. If something is missing, the coordinator asks for it through the portal with a specific question; the organization's other answers are not reopened.

## 6. Review and decision

6.1 **Medical and compliance review.** Every education request is reviewed by a medical reviewer in the reviewing group within 10 business days of triage. The medical reviewer judges the educational need, the independence of the provider, the fair balance of the programme, the outcomes plan and the reasonableness of the budget, including any meal or hospitality line. Charitable contributions skip medical review.

Compliance reviews a request where any compliance flag is raised: a "yes" to any conflict of interest question (Policy 7.3); a screening possible match; a recipient owned or controlled by a government, or whose officers are public officials; or a country rule that needs a prior filing. Compliance completes its review within 5 business days. For a government-affiliated recipient outside the US, Compliance completes enhanced due diligence: a business-purpose note, confirmation that no tender or purchasing decision involving Halden is pending, confirmation that the request did not originate with an official, and confirmation that the payee account is held in the institution's own name.

Compliance review is mandatory for every request from France, whether or not a flag is raised (company choice recorded in workflow v1, approved by the process owner on 24 October 2026).

6.2 **The committee decides.** Funding decisions are made by the Grants and Donations Committee. No individual approves a request alone, except under 6.5. The committee is chaired by the Head of Grants and Donations and its members include the Medical Director, the Head of US Medical Affairs, Senior Compliance Counsel and the France compliance and grants lead. A decision needs a quorum of four voting members, at least one of whom is from Medical and one from Compliance. Members vote in turn and the chair votes last. A decline, a deferral or an approval with conditions needs a typed reason, which is recorded on the request. No member of a sales or marketing function may be a member, attend, or receive the committee pack.

6.3 **Time to decide.** The committee decides within 10 business days of the committee pack being circulated. The pack contains the pre-submission review summary, the medical and compliance reviews, the budget, the revenue cap calculation and any flags. FundingEnvoy may prepare the pack and a recommendation with its basis. The recommendation is advice only; where FundingEnvoy's confidence is below the threshold set in the autonomy policy it shows "no recommendation".

6.4 **Recusals.** Before the vote, each member declares any relationship with the requesting organization or its officers, including membership, honoraria, faculty roles and family relationships. A member with a relationship leaves the discussion and does not vote. Recusals are recorded before the vote. If recusals leave the meeting without a quorum, the decision moves to the next meeting.

6.5 **Single approver for small charitable contributions.** A charitable contribution of **US$5,000 or less** may be decided by a single approver, the Committee Chair, instead of the full committee, within 5 business days. For contributions in other currencies the limit is the equivalent of US$5,000 at the Halden budget rate for the year, confirmed by Finance before the decision. The single approver applies the same criteria, records a typed reason, and is subject to the same recusal rule; where the chair is recused, the decision goes to the full committee. Requests split to fall under this limit are added together (3.5).

6.6 **Conditions.** The committee may approve with conditions, for example removing a budget line, changing an acknowledgement or confirming a date. Conditions are written into the agreement and checked before payment.

6.7 **Exceptions to the revenue cap.** An exception to the revenue cap in Policy 5.2 is decided only by the full committee, for a named programme, with a typed reason, and never by a single approver.

6.8 **Communicating the decision.** The coordinator tells the requestor the decision through the portal within 2 business days. A decline gives the policy reason in plain words. Halden does not negotiate amounts or tell a requestor how to make a request more likely to succeed.

## 7. Agreement and payment

7.1 **No activity before approval and an executed agreement.** Halden does not fund any activity, and the organization may not represent Halden as a supporter, until the committee has approved the request and an agreement has been signed by both Halden and the organization. Both signatures must be dated before the activity starts. For accredited education the agreement is between Halden and the accredited provider; a joint provider may be named but does not sign in the provider's place.

7.2 **Agreement content.** Legal maintains the agreement templates. Each agreement states the amount, the purpose, the activity or programme and its dates, that the support is not linked to any business with Halden, that payment goes only to the organization, the reconciliation and return of unused funds (8.1), acknowledgement of Halden's support (Policy 10.1), and the clauses required by the country annex and, for accredited education, by the accreditor. FundingEnvoy may draft the agreement from the template and check that every required clause is present; Legal approves the draft.

7.3 **Signature.** Agreements are signed by electronic signature by a Halden signatory under the delegation of authority and by an authorized signer of the organization. FundingEnvoy may send the agreement for signature only when the coordinator confirms. Neither FundingEnvoy nor the coordinator signs for either party.

7.4 **Payment by milestone after signature, to a verified account.** Finance pays by milestone as set in the agreement, and only after the agreement is executed and every pre-payment check has passed: the agreement signed before the activity; screening current; for US organizations a current W-9; for the UK the certification in Annex A; for France the filing in Annex B; and the transfer of value recorded (9.2). Payment is made only to a bank account held in the organization's legal name and verified through the payee process in the portal. Bank details are never collected by email, by phone or in a conversation with FundingEnvoy. Payment release is made by a named person in Finance.

7.5 **Suspension.** Halden may suspend payment, or ask for funds to be returned, where an activity is cancelled, materially changed without agreement, or found to breach the agreement or the Policy.

## 8. Close-out

8.1 **Reconciliation.** Ninety days after the activity ends, the organization provides a financial reconciliation: actual spending against the approved budget by category, an explanation of any variance over 10% on a line, actual attendance or reach, and confirmation that Halden's funds were used only as approved. Unused funds are returned within 60 days of the reconciliation, unless the committee agrees in writing to their use within the approved purpose. FundingEnvoy asks for the reconciliation when it falls due and helps the organization assemble it; the coordinator accepts it. For a pure charitable donation where local rules or practice do not allow Halden to ask for a report on use, the reconciliation is limited to the organization's next annual financial statement.

8.2 **Evidence of acknowledgement.** With the reconciliation the organization provides evidence that Halden's support was acknowledged as agreed, for example a page of the programme or a screenshot of the activity website.

8.3 **Outcomes.** For education, the organization reports outcomes at the Moore level stated in the request (for example level 5, performance), with expected and actual numbers of learners and completers, within 120 days after the activity ends. An outcomes report below the level stated is accepted with an explanation and noted for future requests.

8.4 **Closure.** The coordinator closes the request when the reconciliation, the acknowledgement evidence and, for education, the outcomes report are accepted and any unused funds are returned.

## 9. Records and disclosure

9.1 **The request is the record.** Every action on a request, by a person, by the system or by FundingEnvoy, is recorded in the request's history with the actor, the time, what changed and why. For FundingEnvoy's actions the record also shows the model, prompt version and rulebook version used and the person's decision on its proposal.

9.2 **Transfer of value recorded for disclosure.** Before each payment, Finance records the transfer of value with its nature of payment and disclosure category. The transfer is included in each disclosure that applies to it: CMS Open Payments where the recipient is a teaching hospital on the CMS list; the EFPIA disclosure for European recipients; Disclosure UK for UK recipients; Transparence Santé for French recipients. Finance reconciles the grants records against accounts payable and the disclosure files before each submission. A disclosure is submitted by a named person.

9.3 **Retention.** Requests, agreements, decisions, payments, reconciliations and their histories are kept for 10 years after closure, or longer where a law, code or legal hold requires. Disclosure records are kept for at least five years after publication.

9.4 **Audit.** Internal Audit may review any request at any time, with read-only access to its full history. The process owner reviews a sample of closed requests each quarter for compliance with this procedure.

## 10. Roles and separation from sales

10.1 **Roles.**

| Role | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| Requestor and authorized signer (external) | Preparing the request, typing statements, answering conflict questions, signing the agreement, reconciliation and outcomes |
| Grants coordinator | Claiming, completeness, triage, information requests, agreement preparation, reconciliation, closure |
| Medical reviewer | Medical review (6.1) |
| Compliance reviewer | Compliance review, screening adjudication, enhanced due diligence, country filings |
| Committee chair and members | Funding decisions (6.2–6.5) |
| Legal | Agreement templates and approval of drafts |
| Finance | Payee verification, transfer of value, payment release, reconciliation of disclosures |
| Tax | Tax classification and information reporting |
| UK final signatory | Certification of UK agreements (Annex A) |
| Process owner | This procedure, the rulebook, the workflow and their versions |
| Internal Audit | Independent review |

10.2 **Separation from sales.** Sales and marketing employees may not request, encourage, prepare, review, score, approve or be told the status of any request, and may not receive the committee pack. They have no access to the grants system. If a sales or marketing employee is approached about funding, they give the public grants page and do nothing else. A request that a sales or marketing employee started or influenced is declined. For charitable contributions, a commercial colleague may give factual information to the reviewer on request, but has no role in the decision, and the information is recorded as such.

10.3 **Training.** Everyone with a role in this procedure completes training on it, and on the Policy, before using the grants system and every two years after.

## 11. Use of FundingEnvoy

11.1 FundingEnvoy prepares, checks and drafts. It never decides a request, makes a statement on a requestor's behalf, signs an agreement, releases a payment or submits a disclosure. The platform prevents it from doing so.

11.2 How much FundingEnvoy does at each stage is set in the autonomy policy approved by the process owner. At stages that contain a funding decision or a payment release, it may only recommend.

11.3 FundingEnvoy answers policy questions from this procedure and the Policy and quotes the clause. It does not predict decisions, suggest amounts, or write the educational rationale or the needs assessment for a requestor.

11.4 Changes to the rulebook, the workflow or the autonomy policy are made as a new version, tested against the test set, and published only when the process owner signs them. A request keeps the versions it started on.

## 12. Deviations

12.1 A deviation from this procedure is approved in advance by the process owner and Senior Compliance Counsel, with a written reason, and recorded on the request. A deviation found after the event is reported to Compliance within 5 business days.

## Annex A. United Kingdom

A.1 This annex applies to requests from UK organizations, handled by Halden Pharma Ltd under the ABPI Code of Practice.

A.2 Grants and donations are made only to organizations, never to individuals, only to support healthcare, scientific research or education, and only prospectively.

A.3 The title, description and materials of a supported activity must not bear the name of a medicine. The grants desk checks the title and purpose against Halden's product names.

A.4 The written agreement describes the grant or donation and its objective, explains how it supports healthcare, research or education, names the parties, and states that Halden's support will be acknowledged and that the acknowledgement will be **apparent from the start**. Legal uses only the current UK template.

A.5 **Certification before payment.** The written agreement, including any internal instructions about the grant, is certified by a UK final signatory before payment and before the agreement is relied on. The certificate reference is recorded on the request. Finance does not release a payment for a UK request without it.

A.6 Halden Pharma Ltd discloses donations and grants to healthcare organizations on Disclosure UK by 30 June of the year after payment, and support to patient organizations on its website by the same date.

## Annex B. France

B.1 This annex applies to requests from French organizations, handled by Halden Pharma France SAS.

B.2 **Convention declared or authorised before the benefit.** Any support to a beneficiary within the French anti-gift rules is set out in a written convention. Compliance files the convention through the Ordre's platform (IDAHE 2 for the Ordre des médecins; EPS for other Ordres and for the ARS) and decides, by the category and amount of the support, whether it is a declaration or an application for prior authorisation. No benefit is given and no payment is made until the filing is made and, for an authorisation, the decision or the deemed approval period has passed. The record number and filing date are recorded on the request.

B.3 Because an authorisation can take about two months, French requests above the authorisation threshold should be submitted with more than the 60 days in 4.1.

B.4 Halden Pharma France SAS publishes every convention, whatever its amount, and every benefit of €10 or more including tax, on the Transparence Santé public database in the semester filings.

B.5 Compliance review is mandatory for every French request (6.1).

## Annex C. Education checklist

Provider and accreditation; credit offered; faculty selection; statement of the supporter's role; learning objectives; needs assessment; outcomes level and methods; expected learners by profession; other funders; type of support and, for in-kind support, its nature; independence statement for non-accredited education; prospectus; provider conflict of interest policy; accreditation statement for accredited education.

## Annex D. Charitable checklist

Registration or non-profit status; programme description; beneficiaries; use of the donation (restricted programme or general operating); other funders; no-business-benefit statement.
