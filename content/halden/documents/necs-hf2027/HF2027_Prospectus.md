# Advances in Heart Failure Management 2027
## A two-day symposium of the New England Cardiology Society

Title: Advances in Heart Failure Management 2027
Organizer: New England Cardiology Society
Start: 2027-05-14 08:00
End: 2027-05-15 16:30
Location: Boston, MA, US
Venue: Seaport Conference Center, Boston

Heart failure remains the leading cause of hospital admission for adults over 65 in New England. Since the 2022 guideline update, the number of therapies with mortality benefit has grown, but our members tell us that the gap between what the guidelines recommend and what patients receive after discharge has not narrowed. This symposium brings together cardiologists, internists, advanced practice providers and nurses from across the region for two days of practical, case-based education.

Therapeutic area: Cardiovascular · heart failure

## Learning objectives and outcomes

Learning objectives: 1. Apply current guideline-directed medical therapy sequencing in HFrEF. 2. Identify patients who may benefit from device therapy. 3. Plan safe transitions of care after hospitalisation for heart failure.

Needs assessment: NECS 2026 member survey (n=412) and regional readmission data show gaps in therapy titration after discharge and in referral for device evaluation.

Outcomes: Pre/post knowledge test; 90-day practice survey (Moore level 5)

Beacon CME will report learner numbers, completers and outcomes to each supporter within 120 days of the activity and to the ACCME through PARS.
