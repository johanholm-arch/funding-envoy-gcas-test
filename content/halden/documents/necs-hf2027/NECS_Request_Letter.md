The symposium is jointly provided with Beacon CME, which is accredited by the ACCME and is responsible for the educational content, the selection of faculty and the use of commercial support. NECS contributes its programme chairs to the Beacon CME planning committee. We expect around 320 learners, most of them practising in New England.

Our members told us in our 2026 survey that the hardest part of heart failure care is not knowing what to prescribe but getting patients onto the right therapies and doses after they leave hospital. The programme is built around that gap, with case workshops and a 90-day practice survey to see whether practice changes.
