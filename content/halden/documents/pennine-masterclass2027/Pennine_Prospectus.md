# Northern Heart Failure Nurses' Masterclass 2027

Title: Northern Heart Failure Nurses' Masterclass 2027
Organizer: Pennine Heart Failure Society
Start: 2027-03-18 09:00
End: 2027-03-18 17:00
Location: Leeds, GB
Format: In person
Accredited provider: Pennine Heart Failure Society
Accreditor: Federation of the Royal Colleges of Physicians of the UK (CPD approval)
Credits: 6 CPD credits
Expected learners: 80
Audience breakdown: 70 nurses; 10 pharmacists

Heart failure specialist nurses in the North of England run most titration clinics in the region, often alone. The 2025 National Heart Failure Audit showed wide variation between services in time to optimal therapy. This masterclass is for nurses who run or are about to run a titration clinic.
