# Journées Francophones de l'Insuffisance Cardiaque 2027

Title: Journées Francophones de l'Insuffisance Cardiaque 2027
Organizer: AFIC
Start: 2027-04-08 09:00
End: 2027-04-09 17:00
Location: Lyon, FR
Format: In person
Credits: None (non-accredited)
Expected learners: 260
Audience breakdown: 150 cardiologists; 70 general practitioners; 40 nurses

Le comité scientifique de l'AFIC est seul responsable du programme. Supporters have no role in planning, faculty or content.
