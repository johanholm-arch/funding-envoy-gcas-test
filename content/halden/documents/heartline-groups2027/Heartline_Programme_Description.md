# Living Well with Heart Failure: Community Support Groups 2027

Programme: Twelve monthly sessions in person and online, with a volunteer facilitator and a nurse guest twice a year. Topics are set by members.
Beneficiaries: About 150 patients and carers across 6 groups.
Use of donation: Restricted to the support group programme
Other funders: Local community foundation (committed, USD 6,000)
Start: 2027-04-01
End: 2027-12-31

Each group meets for 90 minutes on a weekday evening. Four groups meet in community centres in Philadelphia with step-free access and hearing loops; two meet online for members who cannot travel. Facilitators complete a six-hour training course and a refresher each year.

We measure what members tell us: attendance, a short feedback card twice a year, and the number of members who say they feel more confident managing their condition. We do not collect clinical or treatment information.
