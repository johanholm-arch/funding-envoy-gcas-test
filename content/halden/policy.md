*Halden Pharma is a fictional company. This document is demonstration content for FundingEnvoy.*

## 1. Purpose

1.1 Halden Pharma supports independent medical education and charitable work because better-educated clinicians and stronger communities improve patient care. This policy says what Halden will and will not fund, and the conditions every grant and donation must meet.

1.2 Halden's support is given to advance education or a charitable purpose. It is never given to win, keep or reward business.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy covers independent medical education grants (accredited and non-accredited) and charitable contributions made by Halden Pharma Inc., Halden Pharma Ltd and Halden Pharma France SAS.

2.2 Research grants, sponsorships with a commercial return, memberships, product donations and patient-assistance donations are governed by separate policies.

2.3 In this policy:

- **grant** means funding for an independent medical education activity planned and controlled by the recipient;
- **charitable contribution** means a donation to a registered non-profit for a charitable purpose, with nothing given in return except acknowledgement;
- **provider** means the organization that plans and delivers an education activity and controls its content, whether or not it is accredited;
- **healthcare professional** means anyone who can prescribe, dispense, purchase, recommend or administer a medicine, or influence those decisions;
- **public official** includes employees of public hospitals, public universities and government health bodies, in every country.

## 3. Who we fund

3.1 **Eligibility: organizations only.** Halden funds organizations, never individuals. Eligible organizations are:

- medical and scientific societies and professional associations;
- hospitals and health systems, including teaching hospitals;
- universities and medical schools;
- patient organizations;
- registered charities and non-profit organizations;
- accredited continuing education providers and medical education companies, for education they plan and control.

The organization must exist as a legal entity in its country, hold the registration or tax status that applies to it, and be able to sign an agreement and receive payment into an account in its own name.

3.2 **Not eligible.** Halden does not fund individuals (including a healthcare professional applying in a personal capacity or through a practice that is in substance the individual), government bodies acting in their official capacity, political parties or campaigns, religious activities, organizations that are excluded, debarred or sanctioned, or organizations that discriminate (8.1).

## 4. Principles

4.1 **A legitimate purpose.** Every grant supports bona fide education or a genuine charitable purpose. In the UK and in Europe, Halden supports only healthcare, scientific research or education.

4.2 **Not conditioned on business.** No grant or donation is offered, given or requested in return for, or to influence, any purchase, prescription, recommendation, formulary listing, tender, referral or other business with Halden. Decisions do not consider the volume or value of any business between Halden and the requesting organization or its members. The requestor confirms this in writing, and the reviewer confirms it again.

4.3 **Prospective only.** Halden does not fund an activity that has already started or reimburse a cost already incurred.

4.4 **Objective, recorded criteria.** Requests are decided against the criteria in this policy and the procedure, written down before the request arrives. Every decision and its reason are recorded.

## 5. How much we fund

5.1 **Amounts follow the budget.** The amount requested should not exceed the itemized budget for the activity or the part of it Halden is asked to fund. Halden may fund part of a request. Halden does not tell an organization how much to ask for.

5.2 **Revenue cap.** Cumulative funding from Halden to any one organization in a calendar year may not exceed **25% of that organization's annual gross revenue**. The calculation counts the amount requested, plus any other Halden requests from the organization that are pending or approved in the same calendar year, divided by the organization's annual gross revenue from its most recent financial statement. Exceptions for named programmes are decided by the full committee with a recorded reason.

5.3 **Overhead.** Reasonable administration and management costs may be included, but Halden expects them to be shown as separate budget lines and to be proportionate to the activity.

5.4 **Multi-year and repeat support.** Halden decides each year's support separately. A commitment covering more than one year is approved once, then re-confirmed each year against this policy, the revenue cap and the organization's current documents, and disclosed for the year in which each payment is made. Past support creates no expectation of future support.

## 6. What we do not fund

6.1 **No funding of individuals, named people's travel or product promotion.** Halden does not fund:

- payments to individual healthcare professionals, except fair-market faculty fees decided and paid by the provider;
- travel, lodging, registration or other expenses of named individuals to attend an event, or of learners generally;
- activities that promote, or are about, a Halden product, or whose title or materials name a Halden product;
- company-organized meetings, satellite symposia, exhibits or advertising, which are commercial and outside this policy.

6.2 **No capital or general fundraising for ineligible purposes.** Halden does not fund buildings, equipment for clinical use, or fundraising events such as galas, dinners, auctions or sports events.

6.3 **No gifts.** Halden does not fund gifts, gift bags, prizes or branded items for faculty, learners or beneficiaries.

6.4 **Meals and hospitality.** Halden does not fund entertainment or recreation of any kind, or meals and hospitality for named individuals. Halden may fund the programme as a whole, and a modest working meal or refreshments that are part of the agenda of an education activity are acceptable when the provider decides on them, they are subordinate to the education, they are offered to all participants on the same terms, and they are not the attraction. A separate dinner, reception or social event for faculty or learners is reviewed as hospitality and is usually not funded. Spouses and guests are never funded.

## 7. Independence and conflicts of interest

7.1 **Independence of education.** The provider or organizer controls the content, the choice of faculty, the selection of learners, the methods and the use of funds. Halden has no role in any of these, does not review content in advance, and does not give advice even when asked. For non-accredited education, where no accreditor checks the provider, the organizer describes in the request how it keeps commercial supporters out of these decisions.

7.2 **No direction of speakers or learners.** Halden does not require, instruct, suggest or otherwise cause an organization to pay, invite or select any particular speaker, faculty member or learner.

7.3 **Conflict of interest questions.** Every request answers nine questions, which the requestor answers personally:

1. Does any officer, director or trustee of your organization, or a member of their immediate family, work for Halden Pharma?
2. Has anyone at Halden Pharma suggested this request, helped prepare it, or told you it would be funded?
3. Does any Halden Pharma employee, or a member of their immediate family, hold a role in your organization?
4. Will any part of the funds reach a healthcare professional or official who can prescribe, buy, recommend or decide on the formulary for Halden products, other than fair-market faculty payments decided by your organization?
5. Does your organization, or any of its officers, hold shares, royalties or another financial interest in Halden Pharma or a Halden product?
6. Is your organization, or any of its officers, under investigation, excluded, debarred or sanctioned by a government or regulator?
7. Does any board member or officer of your organization provide paid services to Halden Pharma?
8. Is your organization currently in commercial discussions with Halden Pharma, for example about purchasing, supply, a tender, formulary listing or a contract?
9. Is any officer, director or trustee of your organization a government official, a public official or an employee of a public body?

A "yes" does not decline a request. It sends the request to compliance review before the committee considers it. An incomplete set of answers means the request cannot be submitted.

7.4 **Halden employees.** A Halden employee with a personal or family relationship with a requesting organization declares it and takes no part in the review or decision.

7.5 **Other funders.** The organization names every other company or funder it has asked to support the same activity. Halden prefers activities with more than one supporter and does not seek to be an exclusive supporter.

7.6 **Learner and beneficiary data.** Halden does not ask for, and the organization must not give Halden, the names or contact details of learners, patients or beneficiaries. Reports to Halden use counts and aggregate results only. Faculty names may be shared only as they appear in the public programme.

## 8. Anti-discrimination and anti-bribery

8.1 **Anti-discrimination.** The organization confirms that it complies with applicable anti-discrimination laws and will not use the funds to support activities that discriminate on any legally protected basis.

8.2 **Government officials and public institutions.** Support to an institution owned or controlled by a government, or whose officers are public officials, is allowed only after enhanced due diligence by Compliance. Halden never gives anything of value to a public official, directly or through an institution or charity, to obtain or keep business or any official decision.

8.3 **Sanctions and exclusions.** Halden does not fund organizations, or organizations whose officers are, excluded from government health programmes, debarred, or subject to sanctions.

## 9. Charitable contributions

9.1 **Registered non-profits for charitable purposes.** Charitable contributions are made only to registered charities and non-profit organizations, for a charitable programme or their general charitable work.

9.2 **No business benefit.** Halden receives nothing in return for a charitable contribution except an acknowledgement. A contribution must not be used to promote a Halden product, to reach prescribers or customers, or to obtain patient, prescriber or purchasing data. The organization confirms this in writing.

9.3 **Sales input.** A commercial colleague may provide factual information to a reviewer on request, but has no part in the decision.

9.4 **Reporting on donations.** Halden asks for a short account of how a restricted donation was used. Where local law or practice does not allow a donor to ask for a report on a pure donation, Halden relies on the organization's next annual financial statement instead.

## 10. Acknowledgement and transparency

10.1 **Acknowledgement.** The organization acknowledges Halden's support clearly and from the start of the activity or programme, by the company name only, without product names, logos or messages. For accredited education the acknowledgement follows the accreditor's rules.

10.2 **Disclosure.** Halden publishes its grants and donations where law or code requires, and the organization agrees to that publication: CMS Open Payments in the US where the recipient is a teaching hospital; the EFPIA disclosure and Disclosure UK in Europe and the UK; Transparence Santé in France.

## 11. Sales and marketing

11.1 Sales and marketing employees have no role in grants or charitable contributions. They may not request, encourage, prepare, review, score, approve or learn the status of any request. They direct anyone asking about funding to the public grants page.

## 12. Exceptions

12.1 Exceptions to this policy are decided by the full committee with a recorded reason and the agreement of Senior Compliance Counsel. Clauses 3.1 (organizations only), 4.2, 4.3, 6.1, 7.1, 7.2, 8.2 and 11.1 do not admit exceptions.

12.2 **Questions.** Requestors can ask FundingEnvoy or the grants office (grants@halden.example) how this policy applies to them. Answers explain the policy and quote it. Neither FundingEnvoy nor the grants office predicts whether a request will be approved or suggests an amount.

## 13. Breaches

13.1 Breaches of this policy by employees are handled under Halden's disciplinary procedures. A grant or donation found to have been made in breach may be suspended or recovered, and Halden may decline further requests from the organization.
