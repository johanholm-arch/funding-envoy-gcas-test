Dear Grants and Donations Office,

Heartline asks Halden Pharma for a charitable contribution of $4,500 towards our monthly peer support groups for people living with heart failure and the people who care for them. The groups are led by trained volunteers who live with heart failure themselves. Members choose the topics. A heart failure nurse joins twice a year to answer questions; no company is involved in the sessions.

The contribution would pay for facilitator training, accessible rooms, our online platform and printed resources. A local community foundation has already committed $6,000.

We will acknowledge Halden's contribution by name in our annual report and on our website. We do not share any information about our members with funders.
