6.1 Requests triaged to a reviewing group shall be assessed by at least one medical reviewer and, where any compliance flag is raised, by Compliance.
6.2 Funding decisions are made by the Grants and Donations Committee, which meets twice monthly. No request may be approved by an individual. Decisions and their rationale are minuted.
6.3 The Committee shall decide within 10 business days of a complete pack being circulated. Deferred items return to the next meeting with the open question recorded.
6.4 Members declare any interest in the requesting organization and recuse themselves; recusals are recorded.
7.1 No activity may begin before approval and a fully executed written agreement. The agreement names the provider's control of content, faculty and learners for educational grants.
7.4 Payment is released against milestones in the agreement, never before signature, to the organization's verified bank account.
8.1 Reconciliation is requested 90 days after the activity ends. Unused funds are returned within 60 days of reconciliation.
8.3 Education grants require an outcomes report at the level stated in the request.
9.2 Transfers of value are recorded for disclosure under Open Payments, EFPIA and local law at the time of payment.
